SEO Evidence and Claim Methodology

Public search visibility should be measured from attributable evidence, not inferred from branding, assumptions, or inaccessible analytics.

Epistemic classes

ClassUse
VERIFIED FACTDirectly supported by an authoritative or primary source and checked.
OBSERVATIONDirectly observed in the reviewed artifact or public runtime.
DERIVED METRICComputed deterministically from verified inputs.
INFERENCEReasoned conclusion that is not itself directly observed.
UNVERIFIEDClaim or metric requiring evidence that is currently unavailable.
UNKNOWNInsufficient information to classify.

Search measurement boundary

Search Console query, page, click, impression, CTR, position, and indexing data are treated as unverified until the relevant property is accessible and the data can be retrieved. Access denied is an access state, not evidence of zero traffic or zero indexing.

Claim substantiation

Security, compliance, performance, customer, certification, deployment, and government-affiliation claims require explicit supporting evidence. Brand terminology can describe a product concept, but it must not silently become a factual certification or deployment claim.

Change control

SEO policy is versioned in config/seo/seo-governance.json. The repository validator checks canonical service routes, metadata, structured data, evidence disclosure, sitemap membership, and blocked certification language before publication.

Assurance boundary: ClearGlass uses high-assurance and defense-in-depth engineering practices for this control plane. That wording is not a claim of NSA certification, DARPA certification, government accreditation, or equivalence.
