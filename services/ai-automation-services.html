What we design

Workflow automation, agent orchestration, policy controls, identity boundaries, approval gates, audit records, and operational reporting. The implementation boundary is defined before deployment.

Where governance matters

Automation can fail through excessive permissions, unclear ownership, weak release controls, opaque decisions, or missing evidence. ClearGlass treats those as architecture constraints rather than after-the-fact documentation.

Engagement model

Assess the workflow; map actors, data, permissions, and failure modes; design controls; define evidence requirements; validate the implementation; and establish an operational review path.
